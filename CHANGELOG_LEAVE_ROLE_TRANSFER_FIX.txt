Leave and role transfer correction

- Leave is enabled whenever the current user has at least one other Active membership in the same Property.
- Admin Leave now opens a confirmation modal requiring selection of a replacement Admin from Active members of that exact Property.
- Owner Leave requires separate New Owner and New Admin selections from Active members of that exact Property. The same person may be selected for both roles.
- Member Leave remains a confirmation-only action when another Active member remains.
- If no other Active member exists in the Property, Leave remains disabled. Owner Delete Property remains available.
- No other application features or styling were intentionally changed.
